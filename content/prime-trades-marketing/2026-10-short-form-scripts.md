# Prime Trades Marketing — Short-Form Content Scripts

**For:** Prime Trades Marketing's own Instagram / TikTok / YouTube Shorts / Facebook Reels
**Goal:** Show contractors that you understand their world, teach them something useful, and
make "hire Prime Trades to film and run my ads" the obvious next step.
**Format:** Vertical 9:16, 7–45 seconds, on-screen text in the first second.

> Placeholders in `[BRACKETS]` need filling. Don't put real numbers on screen (views, leads, ROI)
> unless you can back them up.
> Several scripts use footage from the DelaVida shoot. **Get Yonthan's OK before posting
> any of it on your page.**

## Reel 3 — "Every job is an ad you didn't film"

## Reel 4 — "The offer that removes the objection"

**Pillar:** Strategy breakdown · **Length:** 30–45 sec

## Reel 6 — "Shot list vs. reality"

**Pillar:** Behind the scenes / humor · **Length:** 10–15 sec
**Footage:** your shot list on a phone or clipboard, then real on-site moments

- **Format:** split screen or quick back-and-forth, with labels.
  - *"Shot list: 'cinematic slow-mo tree fall'"* → **Reality:** you sprinting out of the drop zone
    with the camera
  - *"Shot list: 'clean audio of the grinder'"* → **Reality:** the chipper starts up right next
    to you
  - *"Shot list: 'golden hour wrap shot'"* → **Reality:** it's raining
  - *"Final ad:"* → the polished before/after
- **Audio:** trending "expectation vs. reality" sound
- **Caption:** "The ad looks easy. The shoot day doesn't. 😅 Worth it though."
- **Safety:** only stage the "sprinting" bit somewhere safe. Never actually be in a drop zone.

## Reel 8 — "Ask a trades marketer" (weekly series)

**Pillar:** Teach · **Length:** 20–40 sec
**Footage:** you to camera, simple background, B-roll from client jobs

- **Format:** answer one question per episode. Pin a comment asking for next week's question.
- **Episode ideas:**
  1. "Should my ads show my face or just the work?" → Both. The work stops the scroll, your face
     gets the call. (Use DelaVida Ad 1 vs. Ad 2 as the example.)
  2. "How many ads should I run at once?" → Start with two different hooks and let the numbers pick.
  3. "Do I need a drone?" → Nice to have. A tripod and a before/after from the same spot matter more.
  4. "What do I post when I'm not on a cool job?" → The 3 shots from Reel 2 work on every job.
  5. "Should I post my prices?" → Talk about how you'd handle it for their trade.
- **Opening line every episode:** "Contractor question of the week…"
- **Closing line every episode:** "Got a question? Drop it below and I'll answer it next week."
- **Caption:** "Ask a Trades Marketer, ep. [#] 🎥 Drop your question for next week 👇"

## Posting notes

- **Cadence:** 3–4 Reels a week. Rotate the pillars so it isn't all selling.
- **Order to post the first batch:** Reel 1 (BTS, sets up who you are) → Reel 2 (teach) →
  Reel 7 (humor) → Reel 4 (strategy) → then start the weekly Reel 8 series.
- **Calls to action:** pick one keyword, like "SHOOT", and use it everywhere so DMs are easy to
  track.
- **Tag clients** in BTS posts (with their OK). It shows them off and puts you in front of their
  followers.
- **Hashtags (pick 5–8):** `#contractormarketing #tradesmarketing #homeservicemarketing
  #contractorlife #smallbusinessmarketing #videomarketing #behindthescenes #treeservice
  #primetradesmarketing`
