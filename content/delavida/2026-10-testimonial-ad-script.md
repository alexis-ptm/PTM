# DelaVida — Testimonial Ad Script ("Walk the Yard")

**Client:** Yonathan, DelaVida Tree Service & Landscaping
**Job:** 20 trees: removal, stump grinding and full cleanup · Halifax, NC
**Offer:** Every full tree removal includes stump grinding — no separate charge, no upsell.
**Concept:** Yonathan walks the finished property with the homeowner and they talk about the job.
It should feel like a real conversation, not an interview.

> **Spelling:** earlier docs use "Yonthan". This one uses "Yonathan". Confirm which is right
> before anything goes on screen.
>
> **The homeowner's words must be their own.** Yonathan's lines are scripted; the homeowner only
> gets questions. Don't feed them lines or ask them to say the offer. Slots marked **[H1]**,
> **[H2]**… get filled with their best real answers in the edit.

---

## Shoot setup

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Cameras** | Cam A: wide two-shot of them walking/standing together. Cam B: tight on the homeowner's face. A phone on a gimbal works for Cam B. |
| **Audio** | A lav mic on each of them. Wind cover. Record a minute of room tone (yard sound, nobody talking). |
| **Where** | The cleared yard, ideally standing where a big tree used to be. Get the before/after angle (B1/B30) in the background if you can. |
| **When** | Late afternoon / golden hour if possible. Avoid harsh noon shadows on faces. |
| **Release** | Signed release from the homeowner **before** you roll. Confirm whether they're OK with their first name and town on screen, and with the house being visible. |

## Part 1 — The on-site conversation (what to film)

Run it in this order. Let it go long; you'll cut it down. If an answer is good but short, ask
"Tell me more about that."

### Opening — Yonathan

> **YONATHAN:** "So, this is the first time we've really stood out here since we finished. What do
> you think?"

*(Let them react. Don't talk over it. This first reaction is often the best clip.)*

### Questions for the homeowner

Yonathan asks these in his own words. Remind the homeowner to answer in full sentences
("Before DelaVida came out…" rather than "It was bad.").

| Slot | Question | What you're hoping to hear (don't say this to them) |
|---|---|---|
| **[H1]** | "What did the yard look like before we came out? What was going on with the trees?" | The problem, in their words. Worry, danger, overgrowth. |
| **[H2]** | "What made you call us?" | Why DelaVida: referral, an ad, Yonathan's quote. |
| **[H3]** | "When you were getting quotes, was there anything you were worried about?" | Hidden costs, mess, damage. Only use the offer angle if they bring it up. |
| **[H4]** | "What was it like having the crew out here?" | Professionalism, safety, communication. |
| **[H5]** | "How did it look when we left?" | The cleanup. |
| **[H6]** | "What would you tell a neighbor who's thinking about getting trees taken down?" | A natural recommendation. Usually the closing line. |

### Yonathan's lines to get on camera

Get two or three takes of each. These are scripted, so he can say them however feels natural.

| Code | Line |
|---|---|
| **Y1** | "I'm Yonathan. I own DelaVida Tree Service and Landscaping." |
| **Y2** | "This job was twenty trees. We took them all down, ground every stump and cleaned it all up." |
| **Y3** (offer) | "With us, every full tree removal includes stump grinding. No separate charge, no upsell. The price I give you is the price." |
| **Y4** | "What matters to me is that when we leave, the customer is happy walking their yard." |
| **Y5** (to the homeowner, natural) | "Thank you for trusting us with this." *(handshake)* |
| **Y6** (CTA, to camera) | "If you've got trees you need gone, call DelaVida. The estimate is free." |
| **Y6-ES** | "Si necesita quitar árboles, llame a DelaVida. El estimado es gratis." |

## Interview tips for Yonathan

- **Ask, then wait.** The best line usually comes after a pause. Don't fill the silence.
- **No yes/no questions.** "Were you happy?" gets "Yeah." "How did it look when we left?" gets a story.
- **Don't correct or coach their answers.** If they stumble, that's fine; real sounds real.
- **If they're nervous,** start walking and just chat for a minute before the real questions.
  Keep the cameras rolling.

## Before you post

- [ ] Signed release on file, including what's OK to show (name, town, face, house).
- [ ] Only use quotes the homeowner actually said, and don't cut them in a way that changes their meaning.
- [ ] If the homeowner got anything for doing the testimonial (a discount, a gift), say so in the
  ad copy, e.g. "Customer received [X] for sharing their experience."
- [ ] No house number or street sign in frame unless the homeowner agreed to it.
- [ ] Fill in [PHONE], the quote and the homeowner's first name.
