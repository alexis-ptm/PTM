## How to use this doc

Every B-roll shot has a code (**B1, B2, …**) in the **Master Shot List** below. The scripts call
those codes in the shot column, so you can see which footage goes where. If you get every shot
on the list, you have enough to cut all three ads and all six Reels.

- **A-roll** = Yonthan (or a crew member) talking to camera, or VO recorded on site.
- **B-roll** = everything else.
- Shoot **vertical (9:16) for Reels** and **horizontal (16:9) or 4:5 for ads**. If you only run
  one camera, shoot 4K horizontal and crop to vertical in the edit, keeping the subject centered.

## Instagram Short-Form Scripts (Reels)

Keep these loose. They should look like they were filmed on the job, not produced. Vertical,
7–30 seconds, on-screen text in the first second, trending or raw audio.

### Reel 1 — "POV: You hired us for 20 trees"
**Length:** 10–15 sec
- **Hook (0–1s):** **B1** wide before. Text: *"POV: you hired us to take down 20 trees"*
- **Beat 1:** **B19** timelapse, sped way up
- **Beat 2:** hard cut to **B30** after
- **Text at end:** *"Stumps too. 😌"*
- **Audio:** trending "transformation" sound
- **Caption:** "20 trees, 20 stumps, 0 mess. Halifax, NC 🌳 #treeservice #beforeandafter"

### Reel 2 — "The most satisfying part of the job"
**Length:** 7–12 sec
- **Hook:** **B22** grinder close-up, raw audio, no music. Text: *"Sound on 🔊"*
- **Beat:** hold on the stump disappearing, then **B24** ground level
- **Text at end:** *"Which part is more satisfying: the cut or the grind?"* (drives comments)
- **Caption:** "Stump grinding hits different 😮‍💨 Comment CUT or GRIND 👇"

### Reel 3 — "Guess how old this tree was"
**Length:** 10–15 sec
- **Hook:** close-up of fresh-cut rings on a round from **B17**. Text: *"Guess how old this tree was 👇"*
- **Beat:** a crew member's finger tracing the rings (count them on site)
- **Text at end:** *"Answer in the comments tomorrow 👀"* — pin a comment with the answer the next day
- **Caption:** "Drop your guess 👇 We counted the rings on this one in Halifax, NC."

### Reel 4 — "A day on the job with DelaVida"
**Length:** 20–30 sec
- **Hook:** **B6** truck arriving, early light. Text: *"6AM. 20 trees. Let's go."* (use the real start time)
- **Beats (1–2 sec each, labeled with times of day):**
  - 7:00 — **B7** gear up
  - 9:00 — **B9** climber up
  - 11:00 — **B12** tree down
  - 1:00 — **B15** chipper
  - 3:00 — **B22** stump grinding
  - 5:00 — **B27** cleanup
- **End:** **B32** golden hour, crew walking off. Text: *"Another one done. 🌅"*
- **Audio:** chill trending track
- **Caption:** "Day in the life of a tree crew 🌳 20 trees, start to finish."

### Reel 5 — "Ask Yonthan" (talking head)
**Length:** 15–25 sec
- **Hook:** Yonthan to camera, text on screen: *"Should you grind the stump or leave it?"*
- **Script (natural, his own words):** "Everybody asks me this. If you leave the stump, it can
  rot, draw bugs and get in the way when you mow. We grind it below the ground so you can
  plant grass right over it." *(Have Yonthan check this is how he'd explain it.)*
- **B-roll over the voice:** **B21 → B22 → B24**
- **End:** *"Got a tree question? Drop it below and I'll answer it in the next video."*
- **Caption:** "Stump questions answered 👇 Ask Yonthan anything about trees."
- **Tip:** turn this into a weekly series. Easy to film, builds trust, and the comment
  questions become the next videos. Film a Spanish version too.

### Reel 6 — "We don't leave until it looks like this"
**Length:** 10–15 sec
- **Hook:** **B26** messy mid-cleanup shot (branches, sawdust). Text: *"Most tree guys leave it like this…"*
- **Beat:** quick cuts **B27** blower → **B28** trailer loaded
- **Reveal:** **B30** clean lot. Text: *"We leave it like this."*
- **Caption:** "Cleanup is part of the job, not an extra. 🌳✨ Free estimates in Halifax, NC & nearby."

## Shoot-Day Checklist

- [ ] **Before anything else:** B1, B2, B3, B4. Once the crew starts, the "before" is gone.
- [ ] Mark the B1 tripod spot so B30 matches.
- [ ] Start the B19 timelapse right after B1 and keep the camera there all day (second camera or phone).
- [ ] Talk to the crew lead about where you can stand safely for each fell. Stay out of the drop zone.
- [ ] Wear a hard hat, eye and ear protection near the saws, chipper and grinder.
- [ ] Record clean audio for chainsaw, chipper, grinder and tree falls (A6).
- [ ] Shoot slow-mo (60/120fps) for B13, B14, B16, B22.
- [ ] Get A1–A5 from Yonthan. Do A5 in English and Spanish.
- [ ] Get a signed release from the homeowner before using them, their face or their house.
  Don't show the house number or street sign in the final edits unless the homeowner agrees.
- [ ] Drone: check airspace and FAA rules before flying.
- [ ] Get B30 and B31 at the end of the day before you pack up.
- [ ] Fill in before the edit: [PHONE], website, any offer, and confirm "Insured" before putting it on screen.
